# New York City region fleet research

Checked 2026-11-09. These records cover New York City Transit subway and Staten Island Railway, plus MTA commuter-rail passenger vehicles that serve New York City: Long Island Rail Road and Metro-North Railroad. This is an operator fleet slice, not every train that reaches the city (for example, Amtrak and NJ Transit fleets require separate operator research).

## Source basis

- [MTA New York City Transit 20-Year Needs Assessment](https://future.mta.info/documents/20-YearNeedsAssessment_NewYorkCityTransit.pdf): A/B Division and Staten Island Railway fleet classes and lifecycle context.
- [MTA 2025-2029 Capital Plan](https://www.mta.info/document/174186): current NYCT fleet profile, ongoing R211 replacement, active classes, and car size standardization.
- [MTA subway loading guidelines](https://www.mta.info/document/152001): seat counts for R143/R160/R179 and R211 fleet context. The CSV leaves capacities blank because guideline passenger loads are not vehicle maximum capacities.
- [NYC 2025 Transportation technical manual](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/oec/technical-manual/16_Transportation_2025.pdf): A/B subway car length references used for length fields.
- [MTA Staten Island Railway update](https://www.mta.info/document/186616): final R211S entry into service and R44 retirement.
- [MTA Long Island Rail Road 20-Year Needs Assessment](https://future.mta.info/documents/20-YearNeedsAssessment_LongIslandRailRoad.pdf): LIRR M3, M7, M9 and C3 classes.
- [MTA Metro-North Railroad 20-Year Needs Assessment](https://future.mta.info/documents/20-YearNeedsAssessment_Metro-NorthRailroad.pdf): M3, M7, M8 and coach classes including Shoreliner and Comet V.

This source set establishes classes and operating status, but detailed specifications remain blank where not verified. R46 remains marked in service based on the MTA fleet plan while replacement deliveries continue; revisit its status as retirements progress.
